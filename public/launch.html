How the burn works.

01
Pairing

Every coin launched here trades against Boron. Volume in any of them is demand for the same token.

02
Collection

Creator fees are collected in the asset they were paid in and recorded on their own line, apart from purchases and burns.

03
Conversion, only if needed

Fees paid in SOL buy Boron on the open market. Fees already paid in Boron skip this step.

04
Native burn

The exact amount is burned against the Boron mint in one confirmed transaction. Supply is read back from the mint, not from a counter.

05
Receipts

Each collection, buy and burn is listed with its transaction, so anyone can check the path from fee to fire.

BoronB5 Independent project. Burning cuts supply, not risk. Not financial advice.
